## Required evidence for every variant

* A precisely matching execution video for the variation (same equipment, supports, range and unilateral side).
* Front and side captures where frontal knee/pelvis or depth is safety relevant, with frame annotations; compare animation to normalized reference paths. MediaPipe/Kinovea/OpenCap can support analysis only when actual usable recordings exist.
* Technical review of shoulder, elbow, spine, hip, knee, ankle, foot/hand contact and equipment positions as applicable. Don't claim 2D joint-length proof establishes 3D safety.
* Written signoff from a qualified youth strength and conditioning/biomechanics professional for the exact media revision.
* App/parent gate and independent review of age suitability and supervision instructions.

Youth supervision principles: [AAP clinical report](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/145/6/e20201011/76942/), [AOSSM](https://www.sportsmed.org/youth-strength-training). They do not validate any particular animation.
