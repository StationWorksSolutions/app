# Galaxy: visual-first learning

## Core experience
Open one deck. See one situation. Predict an action. Reveal and compare. Try one cue in play.

The home is three cards: Game IQ, Ball Lab, and the day's strength deck. Text explains the picture; it does not replace it. Videos, rationale, progression rules and parent settings stay available through optional disclosures.

## Learning design decisions
- Learner-paced storyboards: three states, one short caption each, consistent teammate/opponent labels and pass/run arrows.
- Distinct examples for all 36 concepts. Drawings are illustrative situations, not full formations or uniquely correct tactical answers.
- Three lesson cards: See it, Predict it, Try it. Predict aloud before revealing one plausible next move. No forced typing or external-video detour.
- Levels organise increasing complexity; open access avoids a click-through lock. Application and later recall remain self-reported and separate from browsing.
- Custom play description is a constrained command parser, not generative coaching. Invalid instructions never silently become a different play.
- Strength rotates among four balanced five-exercise mixes by local date and player. Same-day exercise snapshots persist. Rotation creates variety, not a daily lifting prescription or random load increase.

## Evidence and limits
Multimedia segmentation and signaling inform the design; these are research-informed choices, not evidence that this particular app improves soccer performance.

## Next meaningful validation
Observe each child without instructions: find a deck, predict the next move, explain the cue, finish/resume a workout. Record where they hesitate or abandon. On a later day, use a changed situation to check transfer rather than asking whether they recognise yesterday's animation.

Success measures: independent task completion, a useful explanation of a new situation, and returning voluntarily. Time in the app and streak length alone are not learning evidence.

## Still required before claiming broad release readiness
Mobile/browser visual QA, actual touch-device swipe testing, coach review of tactical diagrams, verification of external demonstrations, real family accounts and cross-device sync. No background development task has been created.
